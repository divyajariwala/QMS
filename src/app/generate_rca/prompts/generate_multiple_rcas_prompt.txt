ABS ROOT CAUSE MAP REFERENCE (Official Taxonomy):

**HIERARCHY:** Problem Category → Major Category → Near Root Cause → Root Cause

**MAJOR CATEGORIES:**
1. Equipment/Software Issues - Issues related to equipment design, reliability, materials, software, or support systems
2. Personnel Issues - Issues related to human performance, training, procedures, supervision, or communication
3. Other Issues - Issues caused by external factors or undetermined causes

**NEAR ROOT CAUSE CATEGORIES BY MAJOR CATEGORY:**

Equipment/Software Issues:
- Design Issue - Problems from design phase decisions or outputs
- Equipment Reliability Issue - Problems with equipment performance, maintenance, or monitoring
- Procedure/Instruction Issue - Problems with equipment-related procedures
- Materials/Parts Issue - Problems with materials, spare parts, or quality
- Software Issue - Problems with software design, installation, or configuration
- Utility/Support Equipment Issue - Problems with utilities or support systems
- Other Equipment Issue - Other equipment-related issues

Personnel Issues:
- Procedure Issue - Problems with following or using procedures
- Training/Guidance Issue - Problems with training quality or knowledge transfer
- Human Factors Issue - Problems related to human behavior and psychological factors
- Tech/Craftsman Issue - Problems with technical execution or work practices
- Supervision Issue - Problems with oversight, investigation, or accountability
- No Communication or Not Timely - Problems with communication quality or timing
- Company Issue - Problems with organizational systems or culture
- Third Party/Personnel Issue - Problems involving external parties

Other Issues:
- Natural Phenomena - Events caused by natural forces
- External Events - Human-caused external events
- External Facilities/Activity - Issues with external infrastructure or services
- Cause Cannot Be Determined - Root cause could not be established
